## STANDART BLOK (her promptun başına)

```
Use the attached reference image as the exact fabric source. The woman wears a shawl made from this exact fabric. Fabric fidelity is the top priority.
Keep the base color exactly as in the reference: same hue, saturation and brightness. Lighting may create natural highlights and shadows on the folds, but it must not shift the fabric's color.
Keep the glitter exactly as in the reference: same low density with plenty of plain fabric visible between specks, same glitter colors, same tiny speck size (about 1 mm) and the same scattered or clustered distribution. Do not add more glitter, do not enlarge the specks, and do not turn it into a sequin, beaded, lurex, foil or metallic fabric. At a distance the glitter reads as a delicate scattered shimmer, not a solid sparkle.
The glitter is printed on the fabric surface and follows every fold and crease. Specks sparkle where light hits the folds and stay dull in the shadows.
The fabric is thin, light and fluid with soft natural creases, exactly like the reference.
The shawl is a plain-colored rectangular piece about 180 cm long and 70 cm wide, with neatly finished thin hemmed edges. No other print, pattern, logo, text, lace, embroidery, fringe or tassels.
No other sparkly items in the image: no glitter makeup, no sequins on the clothes, only minimal matte jewelry. The shawl's glitter is the only shimmer.
Style: elegant modest fashion catalog photograph. Adult woman, modest fully covered clothing with long sleeves, high neckline, no slits. Real location in Egypt with warm natural surroundings, never a studio backdrop, never a plain black or single-color background.
Photorealistic natural skin texture. Natural hands with five fingers and correct anatomy in relaxed, realistic poses. Hands never touch the hair or face. No plastic, waxy or glossy CGI look.
Vertical 3:4 format, high resolution 4K, sharp focus on the fabric. No text, no watermark, no logo anywhere in the image.
```

## Sorun giderme (gerekirse promptun SONUNA tek cümle ekle)

1. **Renk kaydıysa:** `Match the shawl's base color to the reference image pixel-for-pixel and use neutral white balance on the fabric.`
2. **Sim fazla, büyük veya pullu çıktıysa:** `Reduce the glitter to the exact sparse density of the reference: tiny 1 mm specks with mostly plain fabric between them, no sequin or metallic look.`
3. **Sim kaybolduysa:** `Add a soft side light so the existing tiny glitter specks visibly sparkle on the lit folds, without adding more specks.`
4. **Şal kısa, kalın veya atkı gibi çıktıysa:** `Make the shawl a thin, lightweight, fluid fabric about 180 cm long and 70 cm wide that drapes softly, not a thick scarf.`
5. **Eller veya cilt bozuk ya da plastik görünüyorsa:** `Fix the hands to natural anatomy with five relaxed fingers, and render realistic skin texture with visible pores and no waxy smoothing.`
6. **Tesettür sahnesinde saç göründüyse:** `Ensure the headscarf fully covers all hair, with no strands visible at the forehead, temples or neck.`

Not: Gemini 3:4 / 4K'yı her zaman uygulamayabilir; çıktı kare gelirse arayüzden oran seçilir.
