# AB AYÇAM – Taş Baskı İçin Düz Kıyafetli Manken Promptları (Gemini)

Amaç: Üzerine Photoshop'ta taş (rhinestone) desenleri yerleştirilecek, **desensiz, düz renk, geniş ve temiz kumaş alanı olan** manken görselleri.
Klasördeki önceki işlerinizden çıkan tutarlı stil: sıcak/doğal ışık, gerçekçi fotoğraf, mat-pürüzsüz kumaş, 3:4 dikey kadraj.

## Genel kurallar (her prompta eklendi)
- Kıyafette HİÇBİR desen, logo, yazı, taş, parlak nokta, nakış, dantel olmasın (taşları biz ekleyeceğiz).
- Kumaş mat, düz renk, az kırışık, kıyafet net ve geniş görünsün.
- Dikey 3:4, 4K, fotogerçekçi, yumuşak stüdyo/doğal ışık, el ve yüz doğal.

---

## 1) LİNGERİE / GECELİK (şık, kapalı kesim)

```
Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 fashion catalog photo of an adult woman model (30s, elegant, natural makeup, long wavy dark brown hair) standing in a bright, minimalist bedroom with soft warm window light. She wears an elegant, tasteful long-sleeve satin-jersey nightwear set: a loose V-neck chemise with a matching light robe, in a solid dusty rose color. The fabric is perfectly plain and matte-silky with soft natural folds, with absolutely NO pattern, NO print, NO lace, NO embroidery, NO logo, NO sparkle or beads – a clean empty solid-colored fabric surface so a rhinestone pattern can be added later in Photoshop. Garment fully visible from shoulders to knees, large uninterrupted fabric areas on chest, torso and sleeves, front-facing, relaxed natural pose, hands visible. Sharp focus on fabric, soft background blur, high-end lingerie catalog look, 4K, realistic skin, realistic hands.
```

Alternatif renkler: koyu zümrüt, bordo, lacivert, gri (önceki işlerinizde en çok bu renkler var).

## 2) KADIN GÜNLÜK GİYİM

```
Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 fashion catalog photo of an adult woman model (30s, natural makeup) wearing a modest everyday outfit: a long-sleeve relaxed-fit button-up tunic shirt with a collar, worn over matching straight trousers, in a solid deep emerald green color. She also wears a matching plain soft jersey hijab neatly draped. Standing in a sunlit modern living room with a large window, soft natural light. All fabric is perfectly plain, matte, solid color with soft natural folds, with absolutely NO pattern, NO print, NO texture motifs, NO logo, NO beads or sparkle – clean empty fabric so a rhinestone design can be added later in Photoshop. Full body visible from head to knees, front-facing, relaxed natural pose, arms slightly away from the torso so the shirt body and sleeves are fully visible and flat-lit. Sharp focus, soft background blur, 4K, realistic skin and hands, fashion e-commerce style.
```

(Başörtüsüz istenirse: "She wears her hair down..." diye değiştirin.)

## 3) ÇOCUK GİYİM

```
Create a photorealistic vertical 3:4 fashion catalog photo of a 9-year-old girl model with a friendly natural smile, standing in a bright, clean children's room with soft natural window light. She wears a casual cotton-fleece zip-up sweatshirt with matching jogger pants in a solid heather grey color (or sky blue). The fabric is perfectly plain, matte, solid color, with soft natural folds, with absolutely NO pattern, NO print, NO logo, NO text, NO beads or sparkle – clean uninterrupted fabric surface so a rhinestone design can be added later in Photoshop. Full body visible, front-facing, relaxed pose with arms slightly away from the body so the sweatshirt front and sleeves are fully visible. Sharp focus, soft background blur, 4K, realistic child proportions, realistic hands, kids fashion catalog style.
```

---

## Photoshop için ipuçları
- Üretimden sonra istenen rengi Gemini'ye "aynı görseli koru, sadece kıyafet rengini X yap" diyerek değiştirebilirsiniz.
- Taş desenini **Multiply/Overlay** + **Displace (kumaş kıvrımı)** ile yerleştirin; kıvrımlı yerlerde Warp kullanın.
- Gerekirse ek prompt: "wrinkle-free, flat-lit fabric, evenly lit torso, no heavy shadows".
