# AB AYÇAM – Taşlı Desen Referanslı Lingerie Promptları (v7, 50 adet)

Kullanım: Gemini (Nano Banana) sohbetine kartela desen görselini (siyah zeminli taş deseni) yükleyin, sonra aşağıdaki promptlardan birini yapıştırın. Kadın görseli yüklemenize gerek yok; kadın ve elbise sıfırdan üretilir, desen elbiseye işlenir.

Her prompt farklı manken, renk, kıyafet modeli, poz ve mekân içerir. Taşların net görünmesi için kumaş renkleri taşla kontrast oluşturacak tonlardan seçildi.

İpuçları:
- Taşlar zayıf görünürse sona ekleyin: 'make the stones larger and more prominent'.
- Desen çok yoğun/seyrek olursa: 'spaced about 1 cm apart' gibi ölçü verin.
- Tüle taş istemiyorsanız: 'apply the stones only on the satin parts'.
- Gemini lingerie promptlarını reddederse 'luxury loungewear / nightwear catalog' ifadesini koruyup diğer kelimeleri yumuşatın.

Photorealistic vertical 3:4 luxury loungewear fashion editorial photo of an adult woman (late 20s, fair skin, glamorous soft makeup, long voluminous wavy auburn hair) sitting on the edge of the bed with one leg crossed, hand running through her hair, confident gaze at the camera, in a cozy luxury bedroom with a large bed, ivory linen sheets and plush pillows. She wears a floor-length fitted satin slip nightgown with a deep plunging V neckline, thin straps and a high leg slit, with a long see-through tulle robe worn open over it, in deep burgundy fabric. The satin parts have an opaque lining; she is fully dressed, no underwear visible, no nudity. The attached reference image shows crystal rhinestone (hotfix stone) pattern on a black background. The black background is NOT part of the pattern; use ONLY the silver crystal stones and their exact arrangement and motif shapes from the reference image. Cover the satin and tulle parts of the garment with this stone pattern, as if the fabric itself were decorated with these stones (repeat the pattern naturally across the garment). The stones must follow the real folds, curves and perspective of the fabric, sit on the tulle mesh with the colored tulle visible between them, and stay off her skin, hair, hands and the background. Keep the garment's base fabric solid deep burgundy with no other print, logo, text or lace. Sharp focus on the garment, warm bedside lamp light, realistic skin and hands, natural proportions, 4K.
